The idea

Seven jobs, one subscription.

Independent shops juggle a till, a stock spreadsheet, a camera app, paper posters and social media. None of them talk to each other. AI Store Manager replaces the whole stack and does the analysis you never had time for.

AI that acts

From alert to action in one tap.

The assistant explains why it suggests something, and you stay in charge. You pick how much it may do alone: suggest only, ask first, or run routine tasks by itself.

Detect

Near-expiry alert

Decide

Smart markdown

A discount is proposed from days left and sales speed, with the reason shown.

Act

Learn

Principles

Built to be trusted at the till.

Selling never stops

Offline-first, fast start-up, support for entry-level hardware. Target: a first sale within 15 minutes of signing up.

The AI proposes, you decide

Every suggestion says why. Nothing ships without your approval unless you grant it.

Your data stays yours

Full export, full deletion, no resale of customer data.

Compliant by design

VAT, fiscal adapters per country, GDPR, camera law, food traceability and e-invoicing via Peppol.

One core, many trades

Profiles for groceries, clothing, shoes and DIY pre-fill categories, units and reports. No separate versions.

Open ecosystem

Public API, webhooks, Zapier and n8n, and an MCP server so your own assistant can read your shop.
